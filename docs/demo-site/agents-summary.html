Agents — What's Built & What's Next

hdpm-chat agent layer · status as of 7/22/2026 · for team discussion HIGH DESERT PROPERTY MANAGEMENT

The one-sentence version: we already run agents in two places. Front of house, Sally, Max, and Leesa talk with customers every day. Back of house, the system watches everything — 12 tripwires, the vendor scoreboard, AppFolio webhooks — and a new roster of assistants turns what it notices into ready-to-act items in Slack and text for a specific person. Every back-office agent is an assistant to one of us, not a replacement for any of us — it does the chasing, drafting, and reminding; you keep the judgment and the send button.

Front of house — already talking with customers

LIVE

Answers when we can't — overflow and after hours — and transfers live calls to Zoom when a human is needed. The first voice many tenants hear at night.

ROLLING OUT
SHOWINGS

CodeBox — next up

Automated access codes for showings — connecting Leesa's tour scheduling to the lockbox layer (and the Key Manager), so a qualified prospect gets a time and a door that opens.

What's being added: the API connection to all three — transcripts, audio, and call events flowing into hdpm-chat. That's the bridge between the two layers: every Sally/Max/Leesa conversation becomes reviewable, searchable, and actionable by the back-office roster below (a Max call at 9pm becomes a structured work-order proposal by morning). And the same three personalities are coming to life on social media — the voices customers already know, out front of the brand.

Already running (the back-office sensors)

LIVE
SYSTEM-WIDE

Tripwires & exception board

12 if-then rules watching every work order: past-due next actions, vendors silent >24h, verified-but-unbilled jobs, aging buckets. Nothing waits silently — the daily sweep isn't done until exceptions read zero.

LIVE
SYSTEM-WIDE

AppFolio webhook spine + syncs

Work orders, vacancies, and (new) the Key Manager stay mirrored automatically. This is the event stream every agent listens to.

LIVE
MAINTENANCE

AI triage proposals

New maintenance requests get an AI-drafted triage (priority, category, suggested assignee) as a proposal — a human approves, edits, or rejects. Every decision is logged; that record is how agents earn more trust.

The trust ladder (how autonomy is earned, never assumed)

L0 · ObserveReports only.
L1 · DraftWrites it; a human sends it. ← most agents start here
L2 · Act on tapOne button in Slack/text; the tap is logged as the human's decision.
L3 · Act, then notifyDoes it, tells you immediately. Earned after weeks of clean L2 history.
L4 · AutonomousInternal ops only (dates, assignments). Never customer-facing.
Hard walls, regardless of level: written messages to owners and tenants from the back-office roster are always human-approved — that cap never lifts. (Sally, Max, and Leesa's live conversations are their own scoped lane: reception, triage, and leasing scripts with a Zoom transfer to a human — and with the new API, every one of their conversations gets a transcript on the record.) No agent ever touches the accounting ledger. Owners see one clean summary line per job. Techs never see or set prices. Every agent message carries a "why" line and a one-tap "stop these" button. Promotion up the ladder requires ~4 weeks at the current level with a clean record — and it's proposed to Craig, never self-granted.

Turnover Choreographer

How to read this page: nothing here replaces a person — the roster is one assistant per desk, doing the part of the job that's chasing and retyping. The order of "next" is a proposal; if the room thinks a different agent would save more hours, that's exactly the input we want today.
