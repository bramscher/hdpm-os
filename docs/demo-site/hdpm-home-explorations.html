HDPM-OS Home — Two Explorations

the QuickBooks-home question, answered our way · sample data · concepts F & G HIGH DESERT PROPERTY MANAGEMENT

CONCEPT FWhat QuickBooks' home page wanted to be: the whole company as a live subway map. Each line is a route; each station is a stage with a real count; interchanges are where lines meet (move-in is where leasing and turnover kiss). Click any station to go there. Nobody draws this — it's generated from the route definitions, and the counts are the same live data as every other view.

Leasing — Leesa's line Turnover — the gold cards Maintenance — Max's line Owners ◉ big stations = interchanges red count = needs attention

Why this beats the QuickBooks page: QB's map was the same for everyone and always static — a picture of software features. This one is a picture of your company right now: counts are live, red means a tripwire fired there, and the map is generated from the same route definitions the engine runs — so when a process changes, the map changes itself. It's the training view (new hires learn the company by riding the lines), the TV view for the office wall, and the "where is everything?" view for you. It is deliberately NOT the daily driver — that's the HUD →

CONCEPT GThe personal landing page — a configurable HUD where My Desk is the centerpiece and everything else is widgets you arrange: stats you care about, views you pin, things you're watching. Role presets give everyone a sensible default (front desk ≠ PM ≠ maintenance); the ⠿ handles drag. The Office Map is always one tap away.
