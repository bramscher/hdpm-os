# HABU form drafts

15 review drafts corresponding to the office-wall photographs. Eleven were compared with the original PDF masters in the user-provided OneDrive General/Tenant Forms folder; all 12 relevant pages were rendered and visually inspected. The application summary and three letters remain photo-based layouts with explicit placeholders. Other forms elsewhere in the company library are outside this batch.

The owner-only library is at /admin/habu-paper/forms, linked from Paper Workflows. Each draft supports editable fields, checkmarks, editable long text, print, individual Markdown download, and a combined Markdown download. Edits stay in the open page only. These are independent blank templates, not active workflow cases or signed documents. They do not route tasks, contact tenants, upload files, or modify AppFolio. Original files are unchanged and are not copied into public assets.

The New Tenant Set-up draft retains the previously supplied August 2026 version; OneDrive also contains an April 2025 version. Confirm the selected master before a pilot. The notice master is dated August 2020; its terms are transcribed for review without representing current legal validity. Source-specific review notes travel with print and download outputs.
