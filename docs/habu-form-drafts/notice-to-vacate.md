## Notice

Source transcription with [vacate date] as an editable text placeholder. No deadlines are calculated here.

**Notice terms — source wording:** 30 DAY NOTICE PERIOD STARTS THE DAY HIGH DESERT PROPERTY MANAGEMENT RECEIVES THE NOTICE.

I/We hereby give thirty (30) days’ notice to vacate the premises on [vacate date]. Per the Rental Agreement, this notice is effective for all Tenants of this property. If the Tenant(s) remains in possession without the Landlord/Agent’s consent after the expiration of the term of the Lease or its termination, the Landlord/Agent may bring legal action for possession. Tenant(s) could be liable for court costs, and prevailing fees and have an eviction listed on their rental reference and/or Credit Report.

It is agreed and understood that the premises may be shown at reasonable times prior to the expiration of this notice after the Landlord/Agent issues appropriate notice to enter. Tenant(s) hereby certifies that payments of all utilities are current and tenants will continue to pay utility fees until the date keys are returned to High Desert Property Management at the office.

Owner/Agent will write one check payable jointly to all Tenants. Tenants should agree among themselves on how to split up any security deposit refund. Owner/Agent may deduct for any damages or monetary defaults caused or allowed by Tenants and/or Tenants’ guests from the beginning of the tenancy until possession is returned to Owner/Agent, regardless of the move-in and move-out dates of Tenants. Tenant Property: All tenants agree that all personal belongings left on the premises of the property may be sold or disposed of by the Landlord without complying with the requirements of ORS 90.425(1) to (27).

This notice is for ALL tenants and all tenants have signed below.
