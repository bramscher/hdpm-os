# Technician invoice drafts

September 21, 2026.

Field staff, including Alberto, can turn completed paper work records into invoice drafts in Work & Billing. An estimate is not required for already-authorized repairs.

1. Open Work & Billing and choose Quick invoice for the work order, or enter the completed work through the existing invoice form.
2. Enter the actual work date, labor, materials, and notes. Save as Draft; autosave also works.
3. Reopen your own draft from Invoices to make corrections.
4. Office staff open the draft from the Invoices tab, review scope and charges, and generate the final invoice PDF. The draft remains visible in the existing invoice list; saving it does not send a notification or imply approval.

Field staff cannot generate the final PDF, change invoice status, create credits, duplicate/delete invoices, or edit another person's invoice or an issued invoice. Invoices linked to approved workspace tasks continue through workspace review and billing. The server enforces these boundaries and restricts field updates to editable draft fields; ownership and draft status are checked again at write time.

No account promotion or database migration is required. Existing office invoice permissions remain unchanged.
